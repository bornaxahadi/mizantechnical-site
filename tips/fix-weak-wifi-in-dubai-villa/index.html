How to fix weak Wi-Fi in a Dubai villa

Concrete and block walls, long corridors and multiple floors make Wi-Fi weak in many Dubai homes. A faster internet package rarely helps, because the problem is coverage, not speed.

Move the router out of the cupboard

Routers are often installed inside a DB or media cabinet near the entrance. Metal doors and walls block much of the signal. If possible, move it to an open, central spot.

Add a wired access point

The most reliable fix is a cable from the router to an access point on the weak side of the house or on the upper floor. A wired access point gives full speed where you need it.

Use mesh where cables are not possible

Mesh systems pass the signal between units without new cables. They work well in villas when units are placed within good range of each other.
